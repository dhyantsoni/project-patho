Dhyan Soni is a rising junior at Del Norte High School interested in all things AI, cyber, and robotics. He leads a careful and deliberate effort to raise awareness of rare diseases. Having lived with a rare disease himself, he understands firsthand how invisible these conditions can feel to the people living with them, and he channels that experience into building media that gives patients and families the visibility and understanding they often lack.
